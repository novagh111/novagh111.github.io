Statement

Flight is the most precise form of controlled velocity. Every image in this collection was made at the intersection of physics and light — in the milliseconds before touchdown, at the apex of a departure climb, or suspended in the cold silence above a cloud deck at 41,000 feet. These are not photographs of machines. They are portraits of intention.
